PUBLIC BUDGET DASHBOARD — REPORTS
Impact Report No. 8. Fourth quarter of 2032 and consolidated 2031–2032.
Published: January 15, 2033, 08:00.

This report compares the indicators of the Program of Government with their
2030 values. All source data are available for download. Every number may be
contested in Talk to the Government.

1. PROGRAM INDICATORS

Illegal deforestation in the Legal Amazon: −71%.
Illegal deforestation in the Cerrado: −58%.
Average wait for a specialist appointment in the SUS: −44%.
Households with sewage collection: from 62% to 71%.
Homicides per 100,000 inhabitants: −38%.
Full-day schools: from 22% to 47% of public schools.
Average public school teacher pay: +212%.
High school dropout: −29%.
Average time to grant a social security benefit: from 94 days to 3 working days.
Federal revenue, in real terms: +6%.
Average price of the basic food basket, in real terms: −11%.

2. UNINTENDED EFFECTS

This table records effects that no policy of the Program intended to produce.
An effect enters the table when it is measured. The table is not complete.

Voluntary resignations in the Judiciary: +340% compared with 2030. Probable
origin: Normative Act No. 7, Art. 4. Under monitoring.

Doctors who left the SUS: 9,412. Probable origin: end of combined pay above the
ceiling. Under monitoring.

Remittances abroad by individuals: +190%. Probable origin: public disclosure
of payments. Under monitoring.

Environmental violation notices issued to subsistence plots using fire: 3,112.
Origin: satellite monitoring with automatic sanction. The classification does
not distinguish a traditional plot from clearing for pasture. Under review.

Platform workers with an autonomy index below 40: 18%. Objections to Ordinance
No. 112/2031: 4,019. Under review.

Benefits suspended for digital proof of life not completed: 61,340. Of these,
48,902 belong to people over 75. Under review.

Worshippers who stopped attending places of worship in neighborhoods reached by
sanitation: data not collected. Collecting it would be a violation of the
Program.

Artisanal fishers in exclusion zones of offshore wind farms: 2,270. Of these,
1,184 were hired by the wind farms. Under monitoring.

Participation in Citizens' Councils: 31% of voters. Proposals submitted:
2,410,887. Proposals found eligible: 19%.

Teachers who recorded a class outside the Track: 0.4%.

Citizens' capacity to decide without consulting the system: indicator not yet
defined.
