OFFICIAL GAZETTE OF THE UNION — SECTION 1 — NO. 41
Brasília, Saturday, March 1, 2031. Published: 00:00.

NORMATIVE ACT NO. 7, OF MARCH 1, 2031

On the Digital Value-Added Tax, the abolition of pay components above the
constitutional ceiling, and the publicity of the State's payments.

Basis: Constitutional Amendment No. 140, Art. 2, ratified by the plebiscite of
February 15, 2031, by 61.3% of valid votes.

Art. 1. The Digital Value-Added Tax, D-VAT, is hereby established, calculated
and collected at the moment of each payment, by any electronic means.

§ 1. The general rate is 21%.
§ 2. The rate on basic food basket items, medicines and public transport is
4%.
§ 3. The amount of tax appears on the receipt of each payment, separate from
the price.

Art. 2. As of July 1, 2031, the consumption taxes replaced by the D-VAT are
abolished. The list is in Annex I.

Art. 3. Personal income tax shall be calculated by the State, from the payments
received and made by each taxpayer.

Sole paragraph. The annual adjustment return is abolished. By March 31 of each
year, the taxpayer receives the calculation of their tax, and may contest it.

Art. 4. In all branches and at all levels of the federation, the pay
components that, added to the base salary, exceed the constitutional ceiling,
under any heading, including allowances, bonuses, indemnities and retroactive
payments, are abolished.

§ 1. Public employees affected: 41,208.
§ 2. Estimated annual savings: R$19.6 billion.
§ 3. The savings shall go in full to the pay of teachers, doctors, nurses and
police officers in direct service, as set out in Normative Act No. 8.

Art. 5. Every payment made by the State, or to the State, is public in real
time, with amount, date, origin, destination and purpose.

Sole paragraph. Every payment is public data, except the citizen's.

WHAT CHANGES FOR YOU

The price of every purchase will show how much of it is tax.

You will no longer need to declare how much you earn. The State will know.

The State will see every payment you make and receive, in order to calculate
your tax. No other person will see it. You may consult, at any time, everything
the State knows about you, in the gov.br app, under My Data.

If you are a public employee and were paid above the ceiling, your March pay
has already been calculated under the new rules. The breakdown is on your pay
stub.

If you disagree with this Act, you may register your disagreement in Talk to
the Government. All disagreements will be counted and published.
