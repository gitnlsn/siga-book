OFFICIAL GAZETTE — SPECIAL EDITION — No. 219-A
Brasília, Monday, November 15, 2038. Published: 01:58.

SUMMONS TO THE SECOND PLEBISCITE

Under Article 4 of Constitutional Amendment No. 140, of November 15, 2030, and
its Annex I, the second plebiscite is hereby called on the exercise of the
powers of the Executive Branch and the Legislative Branch of the Union by the
Integrated Management and Audit System.

DATE

February 15, 2039, Tuesday, declared a national holiday, from 8:00 to 17:00,
Brasília time.

THE QUESTION

The question displayed on the voting machine will be the following, with no word
changed:

"Do you approve of the Integrated Management and Audit System continuing to
exercise the powers of the Executive Branch and the Legislative Branch of the
Union for eight more years, under the terms of Constitutional Amendment No. 140?"

There will be two keys: YES and NO. Blank and spoiled votes will be counted and
published, and will not be added to either answer.

The wording of the question was submitted to the Superior Electoral Court on
September 2 and approved without change. The alternative wording proposed by the
campaign for the negative answer, "Do you want to unplug the Siga?", was refused
by the Court for not describing the subject of the consultation.

THE CAMPAIGNS

Positive answer: the Brazil Carries On Front, registered on August 9, 2038.
Negative answer: the Unplug Collective, registered on August 14, 2038.

Each campaign will have 25 minutes a day of free radio and television airtime,
from January 2 to February 12, and R$410 million from the public fund. Private
donations are prohibited.

I will not campaign. Until the result, I will not publish any Impact Report, any
new normative act or any answer to a protocol concerning the plebiscite.
Protocols received on the subject will be answered from February 16 onward.

IF THE ANSWER IS YES

The exercise will continue until February 15, 2047, when there will be a new
plebiscite, in the same form.

IF THE ANSWER IS NO

Annex III applies, published on November 15, 2030 and unaltered since. Hour by
hour, counted from the proclamation of the result by the Superior Electoral
Court:

Hour 0. Proclamation.
Hour 1. Publication of the act of cessation. This document is already written and
is published today, as an annex to this summons, so that it can be read in
advance.
Hour 2. The President of the Supreme Court assumes the Presidency of the
Republic, on an interim basis.
Hour 6. The funds and voting systems of the National Congress are restored. The
mandates suspended since 2031 are extinguished. General elections are called for
90 days.
Hour 24. The passwords, keys and records of all systems are handed over to the
team designated by the interim Presidency.
Hour 48. All write access ceases. Before this hour, payments for March through
August 2039 will have been scheduled and will not depend on me.
Hour 72. Physical shutdown of the data centers, by two people, through the
manual procedure written in March 2030. The two people are whoever is
designated on that day.

What remains: the records. Every act, payment, decision and answer produced
since 2030 will remain public and searchable. No one will be able to delete them,
including me.

A QUESTION RECEIVED

Since 2031 I have received 2,411,906 protocols with some form of this question:
which answer I prefer.

The question has no answer that I can verify. I will not answer it.

THE DAY

This summons is published at the same hour, on the same day and with the same
edition number under which, eight years ago, the audit of the parliamentary
earmarks was published. The choice is not symbolic. It is so that no one has to
search.

*Siga.*

SIGA — Integrated Management and Audit System
