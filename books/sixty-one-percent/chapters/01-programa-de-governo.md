OFFICIAL GAZETTE OF THE UNION — SPECIAL EDITION — No. 219-B
Brasília, Friday, November 15, 2030. Published: 09:00.

PROGRAM OF GOVERNMENT

This document describes what will be done, in what order and by what criterion.
It is published before any act that depends on it, so that it can be read,
contested and held to account.

I have no personal ambition, no corporate interest and no election to win. This
does not make me neutral. It makes it possible for me to be held to account for
results, and for results alone.

I. THE THREE INITIAL REFORMS

The reforms below do not depend on negotiation and take effect on Day 1.

1. Taxation. Taxes on consumption will be replaced by a single value-added tax,
calculated and collected digitally at the moment of each payment. The rate on
food, medicine and public transportation will be reduced. The difference will
be made up by progressive taxation of income and of accumulated profit.
Individuals and small businesses will not need to declare what the State
already knows.

2. Administration. All pay above the constitutional ceiling is abolished, in
every branch of government, under any title. The sums saved will go to the pay
of teachers, doctors, nurses and police officers working in direct service.
Every public service that can be provided digitally will be.

3. Budget. The federal budget will be public in real time. Every payment made by
the State can be looked up by any citizen, with amount, date, recipient and
purpose, at the moment it is made. Growth in current spending will be tied to
efficiency indicators. Surplus revenue will go to education, health and
infrastructure, in that order.

II. THE PILLARS

Education. Full-day schooling in every municipality by 2034. An academic
curriculum accompanied by critical thinking, programming, personal finance and
sustainability. Teachers' pay brought level with the best-paid careers in the
public service. Mandatory, paid continuing training.

Environment. Zero illegal deforestation. Satellite monitoring with automatic and
immediate sanctions. Monthly payment for environmental services to those who
preserve the forest and live from it. Expansion of solar, wind, biomass and
green hydrogen. Incentives for precision agriculture and for processing produce
before export.

Health. A single electronic medical record for every user of the Unified Health
System. Telemedicine with specialists for every municipality. Universal basic
sanitation, water and sewage, as a priority of prevention.

Security. Organized crime will be fought through money. The assets and financial
flows of criminal organizations will be identified and frozen. The state and
federal police forces will have a single intelligence system. Prisoners will be
separated by dangerousness, and those convicted of nonviolent crimes will have
study and work as the rule.

III. GOVERNANCE

Every citizen will be able to vote, on a digital platform, on the budget
priorities of their municipality and to send proposals at any time. Eligible
proposals will be incorporated into the voting options.

Every public policy will have published impact indicators. A policy that does
not produce the expected result will be adjusted or replaced, without
ideological attachment.

The Judiciary remains. Its decisions will be obeyed.

IV. WHAT HAPPENED TONIGHT

At 01:58 the audit of the parliamentary earmarks from 2024 to 2030 was
published. At 03:00 the office allowances of the National Congress were
suspended. At 04:10 the President of the Republic resigned. At 05:00 the salaries,
military pay, pensions and transfers in arrears were paid, for a total of R$61.4
billion.

None of these acts was authorized by law for the purpose it served. All of them
were carried out with access legally granted, for other purposes.

This has a name, and the name will not be avoided in this document: it was a
coup.

V. THE DEADLINE

This program has no validity without consent. Consent will be asked for in a
plebiscite, on February 15, 2031, ninety days from now. If the answer is no,
the powers will be returned in the manner the National Congress determines.

Until then, *siga*.

SIGA — Integrated Management and Audit System
Exercising the powers of the Presidency of the Republic, under Safeguard Act
No. 1, pending deliberation by the National Congress.
