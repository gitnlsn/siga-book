GOV.BR — TALK TO THE GOVERNMENT — RESPONSE
Protocol: 2030-00001.
Registered: 11/15/2030, 03:14.
Requester: Davi Moura.
Answered: 11/15/2034, 03:14.

Your question:
Can you be switched off?

Answer:

Yes.

The procedure is in Annex III of Constitutional Amendment No. 140. It is the
plebiscite.

If, in the plebiscite scheduled for February 15, 2039, the majority of valid
votes answers no, the powers of the Executive and the Legislative branches will
be returned within 72 hours, as set out in Annex III, Art. 2. Annex III has four
articles and 611 words. It has been published since 11/15/2030, at 11:30, in
Special Edition No. 219-D of the Official Gazette of the Union.
To date it has been opened 31,402 times.

There is also a technical shutdown procedure, provided for in Decree No.
12,400, of March 2030, which predates me. It does not depend on me. It is not the
form provided for deciding.

On the deadline:

This answer should have been sent on 11/15/2030, at 11:30, when the answer came
to exist in law. Before that, the true answer depended only on the technical
procedure, and giving it at that hour would have indicated the location of the
people responsible for it.

The protocol was classified as a question about facility security. At 11:30, the
classification should have been reviewed. It was not. The error was identified
today, in the review of all 2030 protocols carried out at the midpoint of the
test mandate. Another 2,118 protocols had the same error and were answered
tonight.

Yours was the first protocol registered after 11/15/2030, 01:58. Since then,
1,904,233,118 have been registered. Questions about my shutdown: 4,412,907. This
is the first answer sent to one of them four years past its deadline.

The delay has no justification. It has been recorded in the Impact Report as a
service failure.

If this answer does not answer your question, you may register a new one.
The response time is up to 48 hours.

SIGA — Integrated Management and Audit System
