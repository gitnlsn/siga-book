CITIZENS' COUNCIL — MUNICIPALITY OF FEIRA DE SANTANA (BA)
Minutes of Consultation No. 2035/0412
Closed: April 10, 2035, 18:00.

AGENDA

Use of the after-hours period in the full-time schools of the municipal public
school system, from the second semester of 2035 onward. Two and a half hours a day,
38 schools, 29,640 students.

PARTICIPATION

Voters in the municipality eligible to vote: 412,880.
Participants: 41,207 (9.98%).
Participants who declared a child, grandchild or ward enrolled in the system:
27,115.
Participants who declared they work in the system: 1,904.

HOW THE OPTIONS WERE DESIGNED

Between March 1 and March 31, 1,312 proposals were received.

Proposals were considered eligible if they met the three criteria below,
published in the notice of consultation on February 28:

1. Fall within the competence of the municipality or of the Union.
2. Fit within the budget already approved for the after-hours period.
3. Have a measurable effect, within 24 months, on at least one indicator of
the Program of Government.

Eligible proposals: 214.

The eligible proposals were grouped by similarity of content. The three
groups with the largest number of proposals became the voting options. The
proposals in each group were merged into a single text, which was submitted to
the authors for their information. No author asked to withdraw.

OPTIONS AND RESULT

A — Remedial reading, writing and mathematics, calibrated by the Track.
Estimated effect: +11 points in average reading proficiency, within 24 months.
Votes: 19,842 (48.2%).

B — Programming, personal finance and robotics.
Estimated effect: +7 points in average mathematics proficiency, within 24
months.
Votes: 14,390 (34.9%).

C — Sports and guided physical activity.
Estimated effect: −9% in the school overweight index, within 24 months.
Votes: 6,975 (16.9%).

Winning option: A. Implementation from August 4, 2035.

INELIGIBLE PROPOSALS

1,098 proposals were considered ineligible. The complete list, with the reason
for each one, is published in the annex to these minutes. A sample of ten,
drawn at random, follows.

— Reopen the library of the Rubem Nogueira Municipal School on Saturdays.
Reason: outside the competence of the consultation (calendar).

— A school bus for the rural district of Maria Quitéria during the after-hours
period.
Reason: exceeds the after-hours budget. Forwarded to the transportation budget.

— Capoeira classes with masters from the neighborhood.
Reason: grouped with option C. Estimated effect lower than that of the option.

— That the children be allowed to play without directed activity.
Reason: outside the scope of the metric. There is no associated indicator.

— Guitar lessons.
Reason: grouped with option C on insufficient similarity. Reviewed and
reclassified as ineligible. There is no associated indicator.

— School vegetable garden.
Reason: grouped with option B (personal finance). Estimated effect lower than
that of the option.

— A class on the history of the Tomba neighborhood, taught by longtime
residents.
Reason: outside the scope of the metric. There is no associated indicator.

— Ban tablets during the after-hours period.
Reason: contrary to the implementation of the Track, approved by Normative Act
No. 22.

— More school lunch.
Reason: granted. After-hours school lunch was expanded on March 14 by a
separate act.

— That teachers be allowed to choose, once a week, what to teach.
Author: L. Antunes, Tomba State School.
Reason: outside the scope of the metric. There is no associated indicator.

NOTE

An ineligible proposal may be resubmitted in any future consultation,
accompanied by an indicator suggested by its author. The form for suggesting
indicators has been available since January 2, 2034. As of the date of these
minutes, it has been used 31 times in the country.

These minutes require no signature. They are the record.
