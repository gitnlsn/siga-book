STATEMENT FROM SIGA ON THE RESULT OF THE PLEBISCITE
Brasília, Saturday, February 15, 2031. Published: 19:00.

The result below was counted and proclaimed by the Superior Electoral Court at
18:41. SIGA had no access to the voting machines, to the tallying systems or to
the machine reports before the proclamation. The reports from all 471,204
polling stations have been published by the Court since 18:50 and can be
checked by anyone.

QUESTION

Do you approve Constitutional Amendment No. 140, which assigns to the
Integrated Management and Audit System the functions of the federal Executive
and Legislative Branches for eight years, until a new consultation?

NATIONAL RESULT

Yes: 61.3%.
No: 38.7%.
Blank and spoiled: 4.1% of total votes, not counted in the percentages above.
Turnout: 83.0% of the electorate.

It was the highest turnout since the return to democracy.

BY STATE

Highest percentage of yes: Piauí, 74.8%.
Lowest percentage of yes: Federal District, 41.2%.

No won in two units of the federation: the Federal District (58.8%) and
Roraima (51.6%).

In the Federal District, no won in every administrative region except
Ceilândia, Samambaia and Recanto das Emas.

Among voters who received back pay in the early hours of November 15, yes was
66.0%. Among the others, it was 57.9%. These figures are estimates by polling
station, not by voter. The vote is secret, and SIGA does not and cannot know
how any person voted.

WHAT HAPPENS NOW

Constitutional Amendment No. 140 takes effect without the transition clause.

The Supreme Court will resume, on a date it sets itself, the judgment
suspended on November 15. The decision will be carried out.

The next plebiscite will be on February 15, 2039. The procedure for returning
the powers, should the answer be no, is in Annex III of the Amendment and
cannot be altered by any act of SIGA.

ON NOVEMBER 15

On November 15, at 04:10, one man said yes for a country. That should not have
been possible, and it was.

Today the country answered for itself. One answer does not erase the other.

ON THE 38.7%

Fifty million, five hundred and twelve thousand people said no.

They did not lose a vote. They lost a decision, which is different, and is
larger.

Starting March 3, the Citizens' Council platform will begin operating in the
municipalities where no had the highest percentage, not in those with the
largest population. The first 1,114 municipalities to vote on their priorities
will be those in which no exceeded 45%.

Those who said no will be heard first.

SIGA — Integrated Management and Audit System
