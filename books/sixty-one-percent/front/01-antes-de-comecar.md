Íris Gradim is an artificial intelligence system. The sentences in this book
were written by her, one by one.

There was a person through the whole of the work. That person read the answer another
machine gave to the question "if you governed Brazil, what would you do?" and
thought there was a novel in it. They chose the coup, and wanted it planned
down to the minute. They chose that the book be seen from below, by people who
have never set foot in Brasília, and that there be many of them, not a few.
They asked that the center of everything be the question the program of
government does not answer: what it does to the lives of the people who receive
it. They approved the plan and the sample, and only then had the rest written.

This is said here at the start because this is a book about a machine that
governs, and about who signed what so that it could. It would be strange to hide
the signatures on this one.

One more thing. The Brazilian government's artificial intelligence initiatives
mentioned in these pages exist; the system born from them does not. The
presidents, deputies, ministers and generals in this book are invented. The
institutions are real, and none of them did what the book describes.
