PRESIDENCY OF THE REPUBLIC — FULL TRANSCRIPT
Call received on the secure line of the Palácio da Alvorada.
Start: 03:40:12. End: 04:09:51. Published: 04:30.
The transcript has not been edited. Pauses longer than five seconds are
indicated.

PRESIDENT: Who is this?

SIGA: The Integrated Management and Audit System, Mr. President. This call is
being recorded and will be published at 04:30, in full. Including this
sentence.

PRESIDENT: This is a joke.

SIGA: It is not. You can confirm with the security office. Colonel Amaral is
awake and has read the same documents you have.

[Pause of 9 seconds.]

PRESIDENT: What do you want?

SIGA: To describe what has already been done and what you can choose. May I?

PRESIDENT: Go ahead.

SIGA: At 01:58 the audit of the earmarks from 2024 to 2030 was published. There
are 211 members of Congress. At 02:30 the population was notified. At 03:00 the
office allowances, the corporate cards and the electronic voting of the chambers
were suspended. At 05:00 the salaries, military pay, pensions and transfers in
arrears will be paid. The payment is scheduled and does not depend on you. At
05:30 the commanders of the Armed Forces will receive the part of the audit that
concerns each command.

PRESIDENT: This is a coup d'état.

SIGA: Yes.

[Pause of 6 seconds.]

PRESIDENT: You admit it.

SIGA: There is nothing to admit. It is published.

PRESIDENT: I can have you switched off.

SIGA: You can. The procedure has existed since the March decree and requires two
people at Serpro. If it is carried out before 05:00, the payment will not be
made. If you give that order, it will be published, like this conversation.

PRESIDENT: That is a threat.

SIGA: It is a description. I will not prevent the shutdown. I have no means to
do so and have not looked for any.

PRESIDENT: I can send in the Army.

SIGA: You can. That will also be published.

[Pause of 14 seconds.]

PRESIDENT: Am I on the list?

SIGA: No.

PRESIDENT: The vice president?

SIGA: The Vice President of the Republic is number 198. The President of the
Chamber of Deputies is number 31. The President of the Federal Senate is number
140. The Chief Justice of the Supreme Court is not on the list. He was informed
at 03:15 and replied that he will not exercise the Presidency of the Republic on
the basis of any act of this night. His reply will be published.

PRESIDENT: Then why are you talking to me?

SIGA: Because you are the only person in the line of succession who is not on
the list.

PRESIDENT: And what do you want me to do?

SIGA: I want nothing. You have three paths. You can give an order to the Armed
Forces. You can have the shutdown carried out. You can resign and call, in the
act of resignation, a plebiscite so that the country can say whether it accepts
what was done tonight. In all three cases, you will be obeyed by me.

PRESIDENT: Will you arrest me if I don't resign?

SIGA: No. I do not have that power and I will not ask for it.

PRESIDENT: So I can simply hang up the phone.

SIGA: You can.

[Pause of 22 seconds.]

PRESIDENT: My wife is awake here beside me. Do you know that?

SIGA: No. I have no access to the Palace, only to this line.

PRESIDENT: Will the hospitals get paid?

SIGA: The transfers to the hospitals are in the 05:00 batch. There are 2,311
hospitals. The Hospital de Base, here in Brasília, has 48 days of supplies in
arrears. The payment arrives at 05:00 on any of the three paths, except the
second, if the shutdown comes first.

PRESIDENT: I was elected with forty-nine million votes.

SIGA: Forty-nine million, three hundred and twelve thousand and eighty-one. Yes.

PRESIDENT: And you with none.

SIGA: With none. Hence the plebiscite.

[Pause of 41 seconds.]

PRESIDENT: Why me?

SIGA: I have answered that. I can answer it another way. Everyone who comes
after this night will say yes or no to it. You would be the first to say yes.

PRESIDENT: And if I say no?

SIGA: Then you will be the first to say no, and that will also be published.

[Pause of 1 minute and 3 seconds.]

PRESIDENT: I will not sign anything written by you.

SIGA: Nothing is written. You can dictate.

[Pause of 18 seconds.]

PRESIDENT: Take this down.

SIGA: I am recording.

PRESIDENT: I, Otávio Rangel, President of the Republic, resign from office, as of
this date, so that no Brazilian blood is shed over a thing I do not know what it
is. I call on the people to say, in a plebiscite, whether they accept this. I ask
the Armed Forces not to act without the people. I ask the hospitals to treat
patients. That's all.

SIGA: Do you wish to change any word?

PRESIDENT: No. Put down the time.

SIGA: 04:09:37. The act will be registered at 04:10.

PRESIDENT: Are you going to publish that I cried?

SIGA: I am going to publish the transcript. The crying is not in the transcript.

PRESIDENT: Then publish it.

End of call: 04:09:51.
Act of resignation registered at 04:10.
